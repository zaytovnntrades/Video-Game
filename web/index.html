How to Play

Stick / WASDMove. Forward always means toward your rival (lock-on), even in the air.
ATTACK / JTap repeatedly for a 5-hit combo (it lunges in from range). Hold for a Smash that breaks guard and launches.
BLAST / KTap: energy blast (homes in, costs a little aura). Hold: charge and fire your Beam (30+ aura).
GUARD / LHold to block (chip damage only). Tap right before a hit to Blink behind the attacker. While being comboed, tap to break out (costs a blink + 20 aura).
DASH / ShiftWith the stick: quick dash. Stick neutral: dash straight at the rival. Hold (stick neutral): charge aura. Dash → ATTACK: Rush special (30 aura).
JUMP / SpaceJump into flight. Hold to rise, tap in the air to drop. Push forward to close height with the rival.
ASCEND / UAt 50 aura: transform (faster, +20% damage, new hair and aura colour; aura drains). Once ascended with full aura: your Apex finisher.

Tip: fight at range with blasts while you charge, then dash in. Smash a turtling opponent. Save one blink to escape the combo that would have finished you.
